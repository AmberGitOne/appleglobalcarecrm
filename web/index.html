How the calculation works

nobody fills in a timesheetHow people sign in
✓A person signs in on the mobile app, from an email link, or at a desk terminal. The first sign-in opens the shift, the last one closes it.
✓Worked hours, late marks and overtime are calculated from those stamps alone.
✓Under 4 hours is absent, 4 to 7 is a half day, over 9 h 30 m counts overtime.
✓Grace is 15 minutes; three late marks in a month take half a day of leave.
✓The month closes on the 25th and goes straight to payroll as payable days.
Legal is excluded. Counsel are engaged case by case, so there are no sign-ins, hours or late marks against that desk — they are paid against the invoice raised for each matter.
Every other department uses the same rule, so no desk keeps its own version of the truth.

Added on top of the brief

These were not in the request. Each one exists because of a specific way this business can lose money, time or a licence.

Partner scorecards & auto-routing

RFQs go first to partners who actually answer, deliver on time and hold their licences. A silent partner falls down the list automatically.

FX lock & margin floor

Cross-border quotes are priced in one currency and paid in another. A locked rate and a minimum margin with approval stop a quote turning into a loss.

KYC, AML & sanctions screening

Money moving across borders for high-value medicines needs screened payers and screened partners. Screening runs before payment is requested.

Prescriber verification

Prescription fraud is the single biggest regulatory risk in named-patient supply. Licence lookup, signature checks and duplicate detection are built into the Rx desk.

Batch traceability & recall

Every vial traces from partner and batch to the patient who received it, so a recall is a query rather than a fortnight of phone calls. Mock recalls are timed.

Excursion stability decisions

An excursion is judged against the manufacturer's allowance and a cumulative time-out-of-range budget — not by instinct — and the decision is signed.

Refill & adherence programme

Chronic and cyclical therapies are predictable revenue. Auto-RFQ before the next cycle date and a reminder journey keep patients supplied without chasing.

Affordability & patient assistance

Alternative origins, generic or biosimilar options, instalments and reimbursement letters for insurers turn a declined quote into an order.

Bulk & institutional pipeline

Hospital and tender business has different terms, volumes, credit and documentation than a single patient. It needs its own pipeline, not a workaround.

SLA engine

Response, quote, verification, release and dispatch clocks by priority, with escalation. Oncology and paediatric cases run on a faster clock.

Audit trail & e-signature

Who did what, when and why, on records that cannot be edited or deleted — the evidence an inspector, a manufacturer or an insurer will ask for.

Privacy by design

Health data is special-category data. Role-based access, purpose-limited exports with a recorded reason, retention rules and consent separated from service messages.
